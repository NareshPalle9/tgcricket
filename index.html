About us

One association. Every ground.

The Telangana Rural and Urban Cricket Association (TRUCA) bridges the gap between rural talent and urban opportunity. We organise leagues, run coaching camps, and open pathways for young cricketers to reach district, state and national levels.

  • Free talent-hunt camps in villages & towns
  • Professional coaching and fitness programmes
  • Leagues for Under-14, Under-16, Under-19 & Open categories
  • Women's cricket and inclusive participation

Achievements

Milestones on our journey

  1. 2018

    Association Founded

    Launched with a mission to connect rural and urban cricketers in Telangana.

  2. 2019

    First State-Level League

    Over 100 teams from 20 districts competed in our inaugural league.

  3. 2021

    Talent Hunt Programme

    Village-to-state scouting camps identified hundreds of promising players.

  4. 2023

    Players Selected to State Squads

    Multiple association players earned places in district and state teams.

  5. 2025

    5,000+ Registered Players

    A thriving community across all 33 districts of Telangana.
